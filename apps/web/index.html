MengYin Study

A revision and practice app that helps you build your own question decks, drill the questions you keep missing, and see what you have actually learned.

Study official decks or import your course material. Generate practice questions with the built-in AI service, practise code in six languages, and see exactly what you have learned. No ads, no tracking, and no selling your data.

Purpose: MengYin Study is a study tool for building question decks, practising, reviewing mistakes and tracking what you have learned. It is not an AI image generator, and it must not be used to create or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII). Google APIs are used only for sign-in, never for AI generation.
