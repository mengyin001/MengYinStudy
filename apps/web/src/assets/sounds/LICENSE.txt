applause.mp3
============

"A small round of applause" (preview copy), downloaded from:

License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal, public domain dedication)

Used for the 100-point result sound. The app plays the first few seconds and
keeps the rest of the recording untouched. A synthesised fallback is used if
the file cannot be fetched or decoded.
